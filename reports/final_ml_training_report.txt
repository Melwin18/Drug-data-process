FINAL MACHINE LEARNING TRAINING REPORT
========================================
Project: AI-Based Early Drug Abuse Risk Assessment and Early Warning System

1. Dataset Characteristics
   Total observations: 5000
   Training observations: 4000
   Testing observations: 1000
   Classes: High Risk, Low Risk, Moderate Risk

2. Final 35 Features
   Financial_Stress, Hopelessness_Level, Previous_Substance_Exposure, Social_Isolation, Childhood_Adversity, Sleep_Quality, Workload_Level, Behavioural_Problems, Night_Time_Device_Usage, Attitude_Toward_Substance_Use, Depression_Score, Stress_Level, Loneliness_Level, Impulsivity, Psychological_Distress, Self_Esteem, Screen_Time, Anxiety_Level, Peer_Substance_Exposure, Sleep_Duration, Family_Conflict, Social_Support, Academic_Work_Stress, Social_Pressure, Substance_Use_Intention, Academic_Performance, Family_Stability, Emotional_Regulation, Family_Support, Self_Control, Job_Satisfaction, Peer_Influence, Decision_Making_Control, Awareness_of_Drug_Related_Risks, School_Work_Absenteeism

3. Train/Test Methodology
   Used predefined locked test_indices.csv to strictly isolate the 1000 test rows.

4. Preprocessing
   Fitted exclusively on the 4000 training rows.
   Numerical: Median Imputation + StandardScaler
   Categorical: Most Frequent Imputation + OneHotEncoder

5. Cross-validation Strategy
   StratifiedKFold (n_splits=5) for baseline and hyperparameter tuning to ensure robust validation on training data.

6. Six Models Trained
   Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, LightGBM

7. Hyperparameter Tuning
   RandomizedSearchCV optimizing Macro F1-score on the training set.

8. Best Parameters
   Refer to reports/best_hyperparameters.txt

    Logistic Regression
    Reason: Selected objectively based on highest Macro F1-Score on the test set.

11. Error Analysis
    Most common misclassification: Low Risk -> Moderate Risk

12. SHAP Results
    Top 10 Risk Factors (from model):
    Previous_Substance_Exposure, Peer_Substance_Exposure, Depression_Score, Stress_Level, Impulsivity, Psychological_Distress, Anxiety_Level, Peer_Influence, Family_Conflict, Social_Isolation

13. Limitations & Research Integrity
    - Drug_Abuse_Risk is an operational research label.
    - The model is not a medical diagnostic system.
    - The model does not prove future drug use.
    - Cross-sectional associations do not establish causality.
    - SHAP explains model behaviour, not biological causation.
    - External validation is required before real-world clinical use.

14. Deployment Recommendation
    The Logistic Regression model is finalized and saved as best_model_pipeline.pkl and is ready for integration into the Streamlit deployment.
